Biography

I am a Teaching Associate in Economics at the University of Manchester, where I received my PhD in Economics in 2026.

My research lies primarily in decision theory, with particular interests in intertemporal choice and decision-making under uncertainty. I use an axiomatic approach to study how preferences can accommodate behavioural patterns that depart from standard models. My current research focuses on models of non-standard discounting and belief updating under ambiguity.

Research Interests

My research interests include:

  • Decision Theory
  • Choice under Uncertainty
  • Intertemporal Choice
  • Behavioural Economics
  • Real Options Theory

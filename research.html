Working Papers

Sign-Dependent Exponential Discounting

This paper develops an axiomatic foundation for a model of sign-dependent exponential discounting. The model allows the discounting of dated outcomes to depend on whether outcomes are perceived as gains or losses relative to a reference point. Separate discount factors for gains and losses provide a flexible framework for studying intertemporal choice while retaining the tractability of exponential discounting.

Dynamic Preference Foundations of Expected Dichotomic Discounting

This paper studies intertemporal choice under risk and develops dynamic preference foundations for expected dichotomic discounting. The model allows different exponential discount factors to apply over short and long time intervals, separated by a threshold. This structure provides a tractable way to accommodate departures from standard exponential discounting while preserving important forms of dynamic consistency.

Updating Choquet Beliefs through Probability Distortion

This paper proposes a simple updating rule for Choquet expected utility when beliefs are represented by a probability distortion. Starting from a capacity obtained by applying a distortion function to an underlying probability measure, new information is incorporated by first updating the probability measure using Bayes' rule and then applying the distortion function to the updated probabilities. The resulting rule provides a simple connection between Bayesian updating and non-additive beliefs under ambiguity.

Decision Making under Ambiguity and Applications to Auctions

This project studies how non-additive beliefs and probability distortion affect strategic decision-making under ambiguity, with particular interest in applications to auction theory.
